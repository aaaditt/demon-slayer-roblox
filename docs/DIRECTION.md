# Game direction change (owner, 2026-09-19)

Not yet designed or implemented — needs its own spec.

- Replace the separate "story mode" and "PvP mode" with **one very large, Earth-like open world** covering the anime's geography (e.g. Hashira estates, mountains, towns, far apart).
- The story starts as a **tutorial playing as Tanjiro**, which teaches the controls and gives the background.
- At a point in the story the player **creates their own Demon Slayer**, then **spins** to be assigned a breathing style (and possibly other traits).
- More systems of this kind can be added later.

Consequence for current work: combat kits are keyed by sword + breathing style + animation set, not by named character, so custom slayers reuse the Tanjiro template.
